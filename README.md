# E-Commerce Consumer Intelligence Dashboard

An end-to-end **E-Commerce Consumer Intelligence and Analytics project** that I developed independently to analyze customer behavior, conversion performance, marketing effectiveness, and customer sentiment using **SQL, Python, and Power BI**.

The project covers the complete analytics workflow — from data validation and cleaning to sentiment analysis, business analysis, and interactive dashboard development.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

### Customer & Conversion Analysis
- Analyzed customer purchasing behavior and conversion patterns to identify important performance trends.
- Examined customer segments and their relationship with purchasing and conversion behavior.
- Developed interactive Power BI visuals and DAX measures for deeper business analysis.

### Marketing Analysis
- Evaluated marketing-related customer behavior and performance.
- Compared customer engagement patterns across different marketing dimensions.
- Identified important trends and areas requiring further attention.

### Customer Sentiment
- Performed sentiment analysis on customer reviews using **Python and VADER**.
- Extracted recurring themes from negative customer reviews using keyword-based analysis.
- Found that customer dissatisfaction is not strongly associated with a particular age segment.
- Identified **Mixed Negative customers** — customers whose review text is negative despite having relatively acceptable ratings — as a potential hidden churn-risk segment that may not be identified through star ratings alone.

---

## 🛠️ Project Workflow

### 1. Data Validation & Exploration
Used **SQL Server** to validate the raw dataset, identify data-quality issues, and understand the structure of the data.

### 2. Data Cleaning & Transformation
Performed data cleaning and transformation using SQL to prepare reliable datasets for analysis.

### 3. Sentiment Analysis
Built a **Python-based NLP pipeline** using **VADER sentiment analysis** to classify customer reviews.

I also performed keyword-based theme extraction to identify common issues mentioned in negative reviews.

### 4. Business Analysis
Analyzed customer, conversion, marketing, and sentiment-related metrics to derive meaningful business insights.

### 5. Power BI Dashboard
Built an interactive **5-page Power BI dashboard** using DAX measures, filters, and visualizations to present the analysis.
